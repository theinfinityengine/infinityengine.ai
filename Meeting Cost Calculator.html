What to do with the number

If it's a status update
Most of this can become a written post that takes minutes to read. Nobody loses the information.
If it has to be live
Tighten the invite list and the length. Half the cost is often people who could have skipped it.
If it needs real discussion
Keep it. A decision or a hard conversation is worth the room. Those are the meetings that earn it.
How this is calculated
Each person's hourly cost is their salary times 1.3 (a rough loading for benefits, taxes and overhead) divided by 2,080 work hours a year. One meeting is its length in hours times the combined hourly cost of the room. The yearly figure is that times how often it runs. These are estimates, not payroll: the point is the order of magnitude.
